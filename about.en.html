About Us

Discover the story of the site and its goals

Our Story

The website aissasaw.github.io was born from a passion for music, culture and Algerian history. From Oued Ghir, Bejaia, we want to share with the world our history, our art and the memory of our martyrs.

We started with Algerian rap, its history and its voices, then we expanded the site to help young artists choose the right equipment, and to discover the glorious history of our heroes.

What Will You Find On The Site?

🎤 History of Rap in Algeria: From its beginnings until today.
🎙️ Microphone Guide: How to choose the best mic for recording and studio.
🇩🇿 Martyrs Corner: Glory and eternity to our martyrs, like the martyr Tayeb Abdelli.
👤 CV and Projects: My artistic works and experiences.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple: to create an Algerian space, in Arabic, French and English, that preserves memory, helps young talents, and makes Algerian culture known to the whole world.

Thank you for supporting us, and long live Algeria! 🇩🇿
